Important Update Information

Hello,

We're writing to inform you about an important upcoming update to the Revise app. We are excited to introduce a powerful new offline Backup and Restore feature. This has been a top request and will allow you to save, merge, and transfer your valuable question data securely in the future.

To implement this significant improvement, users on older versions (v1.5 and below) will have their local data reset upon updating to the new version (v1.7+).

We made our utmost efforts to find a way to preserve the data through this transition, but unfortunately, the scale of this necessary architectural change made it impossible. We are truly sorry for this one-time inconvenience.

Once you update, you can immediately use the new backup feature to protect your data going forward.

Thank you for your understanding and for being a part of the Revise community.
- The Revise App Team
